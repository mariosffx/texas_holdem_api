## 2. Texas Hold'em poker: hand eval. + probability calculator

- Poker rules (by Peter Norvig), Texas Hold'em (Wikipedia, English/German)
- API functionality: 
  note: a 'card' is specified as string consisting of 2 alphanumeric characters, for example 'HA' for Heart-Ace, or 'S7' for Spade-7, or 'CT' for Club-Ten
  - 2 cards + 5 cards => best hand and its value (eg, three of a kind)
  - 2 times (2 cards + 5 cards) => best hand for each of the two card sets and the winner
  - 2 cards + 0, 3, 4, 5 cards + number of players + number of Monte Carlo simulations => probability of winning, calculated with a Monte Carlo simulation
- test cases for hand evaluation and comparison of hands
- technology stack as in TempConv (Go, Flutter/Dart, you choose: REST or gRPC+ProtoBuf)
